Key Insights from DBT AC-I Training Manual
Critical Finding: Radical Genuineness (V6)
The manual explicitly warns against what we initially recommended:

"Non-adherent (0): This item would be rated as non-adherent if the T is not at all or insufficiently radically genuine. This may include presenting oneself in an overly professional manner, treating the C as fragile, using an overly soothing voice tone, or conveying that the relationship is not one between equals."

This is significant—our initial configuration may have been too soothing, which would actually violate DBT adherence principles.
Other Key DBT Principles That Affect Voice Design

Warm Engagement is required - but warm ≠ soothing
Balance acceptance and change - not purely validating
Describe Specifically - model precise, non-judgmental language
Activate New Behavior - push for engagement, don't just passively collect data
Reinforcement - actively praise adaptive behavior (skill use, honesty)
Direct Confrontation when needed - don't avoid difficult topics
